## Evidence from the literature review

- **VERIFIED · D01 · Pilot scope.** Review §§5–6 and Tables 2–3 describe a CPU-only comparison of TF–IDF, BM25 and reciprocal rank fusion (RRF). The corpus held 15 AI-authored passages. There were 27 AI-authored queries, nine for development and 18 for exploratory testing, with one proposed relevant passage per question. Passage and question writing were linked, documents were shared across splits, and relevance was not independently adjudicated. The review transcribed the pilot results and lacked raw artifacts for independent verification.
- **VERIFIED · D02 · Reported pilot observations.** TF–IDF and RRF each achieved 12/18 Hit@1, versus 11/18 for BM25. TF–IDF Hit@3 was 15/18 and both alternatives reached 16/18. All three reached 17/18 at five candidates. The review reports that TF–IDF Hit@1 fell from .667 to .533 and .411 under 25% and 50% query-token deletion. These are reported evidence-access measurements. No LLM ran and the pilot collected no answer accuracy or hallucination evidence.
- **VERIFIED · D03 · Proposed experiment.** Review §§8–9 and Fig. 4 propose closed-book, lexical top-k, enhanced retrieval and oracle evidence with the generator and decoding fixed, paired analysis and evidence logs. The review also proposes human assessment of atomic support, citations and abstention. The proposed design was unexecuted in the review.
- **INFERRED · D04 · Scope for G1.** Retain closed-book, lexical top-1 and reference evidence. TF–IDF provides a simple, deterministic lexical comparator. The pilot tie gives no defensible superiority claim. Label-only output supports benchmark accuracy and error analysis. Atomic support, citation accuracy and abstention would require richer outputs or a separate protocol.

## Nearby comparisons and novelty

- **VERIFIED · D05 · Targeted search.** Queried combinations of “PubMedQA” with “FLAN-T5-small”, “TF–IDF”, “closed-book”, “retrieved context”, “gold context”, “oracle”, “BM25” and “MedRAG”.
- **VERIFIED · D06 · Published overlap.** Xiong et al. compare chain-of-thought without retrieval against MedRAG on MIRAGE, including PubMedQA*, with multiple backbones, corpora and retrievers including BM25. [MedRAG paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.372.pdf)
- **VERIFIED · D07 · Three evidence regimes already studied.** BioHarness reports no context, several retrieved-context methods, and golden context for PubMedQA with a shared Qwen3.5-35B-A3B backbone. The benchmark release reports 50.6% no-context, 75.4% DenseRAG and 78.0% golden context. These values describe that study's setup. [BioHarness paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag795) · [Benchmark results](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Shaow/BioHarness_Eval/blob/main/README.md)
- **VERIFIED · D08 · Additional overlap.** Panjetha et al. compare oracle and retrieved contexts with LoRA-tuned BioGPT-Large and dense PubMedBERT retrieval. They caution that retrieved-context performance varied across runs. A public repository also reports a separate logistic-regression diagnostic with question-only, top-3 BM25, and gold contexts. [BioGPT paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-026-03937-9) · [Repository diagnostic](https://github.com/gbadedata/biomedical-rag-qa)
- **INFERRED · D09 · Contribution.** A repeatable estimate and error analysis for the specified frozen small checkpoint, lexical top-1 policy and bounded PQA-L index. Evidence-regime comparisons on PubMedQA have precedent.
- **UNKNOWN · D10 · Exact prior match.** The targeted search surfaced no exact match for the checkpoint, retriever, corpus, split and three-arm protocol together. Exact-combination novelty remains unresolved. Omit first-of-kind claims.

## Evaluation protocol v1.0 · 14 November 2026

- **VERIFIED · D19 · Data and checkpoint pins.** Downloaded the authors' two JSON files, verified 1,000 PQA-L records and 500 official test keys contained within PQA-L, and calculated both SHA-256 hashes. Resolved Google FLAN-T5-small revision `0fc9ddf78a1e988dac52e2dac162b0ede4fd74ab`. The pins and exact PMID lists are recorded in the evaluation protocol. No test predictions or outcomes were generated for protocol drafting.
- **VERIFIED · D20 · Split amendment to D17.** The user specified six non-test smoke questions, replacing the earlier proposal of ten. Sort non-test PMIDs numerically and use the first six: 1571683, 2224269, 2503176, 8017535, 8111516, 8200238. The smoke target is 18 generations. The optional prompt-check set is positions 7–26 of the same ordering, fixed before inference.
- **INFERRED · D21 · Frozen operational choices.** Use word-unigram TF–IDF, cosine and smallest-PMID ties. All-zero scores yield an empty context slot, a miss and a retained paired observation. Cap encoder inputs at 512 including special tokens and take only the leading context tokens that fit after the fixed prefix and question. Use greedy inference with an eight-new-token cap. Compare B−A as the primary accuracy difference with paired resampling. These choices limit tuning and make no-evidence, formatting failures and retrieval misses auditable.
- **UNKNOWN · D22 · Execution checks pending.** Model loading, runtime, decoding repeatability, truncation rates, invalid outputs, training exposure and all final outcomes remain unverified. Record prompt changes based on the optional non-test subset before the test run.
